How to Use Hybrid Data Pipeline

4-Step Connected Workflow Guide
STEP 1: GENERATE DATASET

Go to 3. Data Generation, pick a domain (Cars, Housing, Churn, Fraud, Stock Trends) and row count (100 to 100,000 rows), then click Generate Dataset Now.

STEP 2: PIPELINE OBSERVATORY

Go to 1. Pipeline Observatory to inspect Accuracy metrics, Data Health Pie Charts, and compare dataset parameters (e.g. Price vs Sqft).

STEP 3: MODEL LAB & PREDICT

Go to 2. Model Lab, train models on your active dataset, and use the Prediction Studio customized specifically for your dataset (e.g. Car Price, Housing Price, or Churn).

STEP 4: DATA RIVER 3D

Go to 4. Data River 3D to view your dataset transformed into dynamic 3D WebGL terrain waves and light streams.
